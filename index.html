Good questions

Before you reach out.

How long does a website take?

A Starter site typically takes 7–10 days once we have your content. Larger projects with bookings, stores or custom features usually take 3–6 weeks, depending on scope.

Do I own the website afterwards?

Yes. Once the project is paid for, the code, content and design are yours. You also fully own your domain name and hosting account — we simply help you set them up.

What do I need to provide to get started?

Your logo (if you have one), any existing photos or text, a list of pages or features you want, and a few examples of sites you like. We'll guide you through the rest.

Do you help with hosting and the domain name?

Yes — we can register your domain, set up hosting, and walk you through it, or work with a domain and host you already have.

What happens after the site launches?

Every package includes a short post-launch support window to fix any issues. After that, we offer optional ongoing care plans for updates, backups and small changes.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. We'll review what you have, keep what's working, and rebuild the rest to look sharper and perform better.
